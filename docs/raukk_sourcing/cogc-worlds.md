# COGC world lists

Candidate planets by Chamber of Commerce program, transcribed from planet search 2026-08-12. These are the production sites; the extraction pool is in [planet-pool.md](planet-pool.md) and the farm list in [agri-planets.md](agri-planets.md). Machine-readable in [facts/cogc-worlds.json](facts/cogc-worlds.json).

Distances are jumps to AI1 / CI1 / IC1 / NC1. Resource figures are daily extraction per building. A production site does not need deposits, so the resource column matters only where a base can extract and fabricate on the same planet.

## Faction mapping

The in-game faction names map to the code's bonus table as follows, at the fully-used-permits rate where the multiplier is 2:

| Faction | Bonus | Applies to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Antares Initiative | 10 % electronics | ELECTRONICS |
| Castillo-Ito Mercantile | 10 % manufacturing | MANUFACTURING |
| Insitor Cooperative | 6 % agriculture, 4 % food industries | AGRICULTURE |
| NEO Charter Exploration | 4 % metallurgy, 6 % construction | METALLURGY, CONSTRUCTION |
| No faction | 4 % each on chemistry, fuel refining, resource extraction | CHEMISTRY, RESOURCE_EXTRACTION |

Electronics and manufacturing carry the largest faction bonuses at 10 %, but they are also the two smallest permit blocks, so the total gain is modest. NEO covers both metallurgy and construction, 55 permits between them, which is why those two can share players.
