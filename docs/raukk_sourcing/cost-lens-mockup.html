Design notes — what the persona reviews changed
  • All ¤ stay per 1 SF, at every zoom level. The pinned blue context line states it each time you drill in (casual-player fix for “100% of what?”).
  • Tooltips lead with one number — the contribution to the final unit; the item’s own unit cost is secondary.
  • Reconciliation is visible: Pareto total + cycle snapshots = chart root = header, shown with a tie-out line (min-maxer requirement).
  • vs-market delta on every internal node — the “should this branch exist?” answer. BSE is deliberately shown costing more than buying it.
  • Loops (H2O → RAT → H2O) are cut at the repeat and shown as a tinted dashed ↺ leaf whose tooltip explains the converged snapshot cost in one sentence.
  • Coverage is phrased as “self-supplied N% — the rest is bought” instead of a bare badge.
  • Icicle over sunburst/circle-packing/treemap: aligned lengths are the only encoding that shows part-to-whole truthfully at this depth; packing wastes 20–35% of area and lies about shares. Shared materials (the DAG-ness) are recovered through the Pareto cross-highlight instead of a sankey.
  • Slivers under ~1.5% roll into a zoomable “· n more” block; no minimum widths on real segments, no log scales.
  • Production fees appear as their own unavoidable external category (pending the separate fees investigation).
  • Flow tab: a sankey-style ribbon view of the same tree (per-column merge of repeated materials), for the “accounting app” reading. Blocks stays the drill-down view; Flow is the shape-of-the-empire view. Both share the same selection — click a node to trace it.
  • Marginal shortfall pricing: when a plan covers only part of empire demand (BSE at 60%), the covered share is priced at true cost and the remainder appears as a separate solid “CX top-up” line priced at market — so internal vs CX money is never blended in one number.
  • Backing out: ← Back button (one level), clickable breadcrumbs (any level), and Esc (clears trace first, then steps back).
